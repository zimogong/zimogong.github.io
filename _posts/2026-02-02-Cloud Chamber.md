On the last day of this semester, I finally managed to conduct the cloud chamber experiment in my school's astonomy club after several weeks of final exam preparation. In order to clarify the purpose and the function of the apparatus, I first showed the structure of the cloud chamber and its working principle on a PPT document in addition to pictures of various particles detected by the cloud chamber. 
![picture 2](picture2.jpg)
After the audience got the gist of this device, we stepped into the physical demonstration part. To start with, The bottom of the device was filled with dry ice to lower the temperature. Then, a rectangular metal container with a layer of liquid alcohol was placed on the top of the dry ice accompanied by a loud screeching sound made by the cooling metal container. This layer of alcohol is essential to the experiment since the visualization of the particles' trajectories rely on the alcoholic vapor. However, in order to create the vapor from the liquid alcohol, a heating unit must be added. Thus, on the top of the apparatus, a circuit with a heating cell was installed to establish a temperature gradient that facilitate vapor formation. Also, multiple strips of solenoid hangs in the middle of the acrylic box that covers the device to create magnetic fields that can alter the direction of the particles so that their trajectories can be easily visualized.
![picture 3](picture3.jpg)
After the whole apparatus was connected to the power source, alcohol starts to vaporize. Although no trajectory was visible just after the formation of the vapor, they began to pop out among the alcohol steam several minutes later. Some of them were straight and thick, resembling alpha particles that have a large mass and zero charge; some were curved and narrow, which indicates the prescence of charged beta particles. There were also some discontinuous traces dispersed in the chamber that were made by the gamma photons.
The audiences were certainly fascinated by this extraordinary phenonmenon, gazing at the chamber for at least 20 minutes straight.
