  A decent commencement of my popularization of science activity! Today I went to Chongqing's University Town to join the scientific activity organized by the High-Tech District.

 Luckily, I managed to reserve a exhibition stand from the High-Tech District (thanks to my mom) to show some of the items I received previously and introduced some astronomical knowledges to the attendant of the activity. Before that, I wrote some articles that explain the cosmic rays and their connection to Earth and extraterrestrial life so that I could print them out in sheets to hand out to the public.
 
 During the activity, I encountered numerous participants who take interest in the subject, and most of them are children under 10 years old. Those little fellas were very inquisitive and eager to dive deeper into the astronomy, which I found to be very impressive.

 There was one confident and remarkable third-grader who impressed me the most when he stopped at my desk to investigate the materials on it. The boy displayed an abundance of astronomical knowledge that can even surpass most normal adults since, according to his mother, every time he plays with the phone, he browses tons of intriguing articles and videos about astronomy. I absolutely appreciate the boy's passion for astronomy, thinking that since he is still secured by his own interest from the test-oriented education of China, he will definitely become a astronomer or whatever profession he want in the future. 
 ![picture 7](picture7.jpg)

Overall, although I prepared it in a hurry, I think the event was successful, and I believe that later on, if I can recruit more members to join my club, our future activities will become more intriguing and engaging.
