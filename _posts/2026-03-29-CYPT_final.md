After two days of intense competition and fifteen rounds of fierce confrontation, the energetic CYPT competition finally comes to the end. During the two days, I saw the unprecedented strength of collaboration among our team members. Everyone of us devoted all of our spirit into refining presentation slides, giving advice to the team member on the stage, and debating with our opponents. There was euphoric moments when our team won the highest score of the rounds, depressing times when not receiving the expected points, and enraging occasions when an old classmate and "friend" from the opponent team stab us in the back, but overall, I think our efforts of arduous preparation paid off. We were thrilled to be informed that we ranked the 6th among the 45 competing team and received the 1st prize, another improvement compared to our school's grade last year. Moreover, another teammate and I were also admitted by the national training team (30 out of 225 participants), and the only girl in our team won the best reviewer award. The only disappointment was that we were only 3 points away from the 5th team, meaning that if we won 1 more point when reporting or opposing, we could get into the final and won the outstanding prize. Nevertheless, the CYPT competition not only enhanced our physics and presentation skills, but also improved the collaboration among teammates and allowed us to make friends with outstanding students from various province in the whole country.
