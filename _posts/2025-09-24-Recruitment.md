Today our school organized the recruitment activity for the school clubs, and I certainly set up my own recruitment booth along with other clubs since I was the head of the club. Originally, I did not expect to become an founder because I was planning to join the astronomy club that was already established. Alas, when I inquired my counselor about the club, she informed me that the club no longer existed as the previous leader had graduated. Thus, with the help of my counselor, Kiko(since by then the registration for new clubs had already ended and she had to talk to the staff of our school to reserve another spot for my club), I decided to start a new club.  Although I did not prepare for this event properly (since I just managed to reserve a spot for this activity 4 days before it and I still had a lot of school courses and assignments), I did set up a large poster and bring a space telescope for the exhibition so that other students would perceive my club as a decent one.

Initially, there wasn't any student who seemed to be interested in my club, but gradually, some of them began to circle around my spot, scrutinizing my poster and the handouts on the tables (Some of them were just interested in the telescope, and some of them wanted to join the club). Anyway, thanks to my countless explanations to the visitors about my club and the charisma of the telescope, I finally managed to recruit 16 members for my new club. Phew

Overall, the Recruitment was more than a success because I really did not expect there would be 10+ people who were willing to join my club. (Special thanks to Ruibo Wang, who helped me spread the word my club to the 10th graders, and Kiko, who made the Recruitment of this club possible)
