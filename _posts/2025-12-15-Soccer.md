# The Class Soccer Match

For all these 1 and a half years, I've been anticipating the 11th grade class soccer tournament of our school, and I've also prepared for it for a long time, practicing soccer skills with my classmates whenever possible. We even aimed to fight our way into the final of the tournament at first, but our dream got shattered when the match finally arrived. 

In the draw, we unfortunately ran into the class with five players from our school soccer team, which recently won second place in the national tournament. I am 1000% sure that that class is the strongest of all 22 classes in grade 11, so we did not even stand a single chance of beating them. The more devastating fact is that only the 1st ranking team of a group(6 of the groups are comprised of 3 teams and 2 of which contain 2, we were in a 3-team group) can qualify for the knockout stage, so our journey will basically end after two games in group stage.

For the first game, we had our match with that strongest class, and, fortunately, the score of our opponent did not achieve double digits(9:2, only 1 score away). The only two things that comfort me were that I scored one goal against the opponent in that match and the 3rd team lost even more points than us (11:2)

As for the second match, we expected to defeat the opponent without much pressure, but we were wrong. The goalkeeper for the first half of the match was so contemptuous that he frequently left the goal and try to show off by dribbling. Another teammate kept wasting scoring opportunities in front of the opponents' goal, shooting down airplanes at a distance of 5 meters from the goal, which was impressive since its hard for a normal player to miss the goal. Agitated by their performance, I lost patience in defending and tried to join the attack myself, but by doing so our defense was severely weakened. After I joined the attack, the opponents struck back and scored 2 goals despite the fact that I scored one point through a header at last. 
  Thus, we lost again by 2:1.
